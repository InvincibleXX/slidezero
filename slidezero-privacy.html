SlideZero: Block Puzzle – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game SlideZero: Block Puzzle (the "Game") handles information. The Game does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, bricks, boosters, city buildings, purchases you have made and settings (such as sound and vibration) are saved only on your device. This information is not sent to us. If you delete the Game, this data is deleted too.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Some ads appear between levels; other ads are optional "rewarded" ads that you can choose to watch for a bonus. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Apple IDFA, if you allow it, or the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device type, operating system, language and app version
  • information about ads shown and your interactions with them

Your choices:

  • On iPhone and iPad, the Game asks for permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. If you do not agree, you may still see non-personalized ads.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases (for example coins, booster packs or removing ads). Payments are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment card details or billing address. We only receive a confirmation of the purchase from the store so that the item can be delivered in the Game and restored later. Purchases are subject to the privacy policy of Apple or Google.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or health data. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and deletion

Game data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the Game. For advertising data, you can reset your advertising identifier in your device settings or contact Google using the links above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
