Summary

SlideZero: Block Puzzle does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it works completely offline.

1. Who we are

SlideZero: Block Puzzle (“the Game”, “we”, “us”) is developed and published by Kartal Önel. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at onelcan123@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data, and it does not use device identifiers for tracking.

3. Data stored on your device

To save your game, the following information is stored only locally on your device:

  • Level progress (which levels you have completed)
  • Coins and purchased boosters (Hammer, Freeze)
  • Settings such as sound and vibration on/off

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to any third party. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

4. Third-party services

The current version of the Game does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. In-game coins and boosters are earned through gameplay and cannot be bought with real money.

If a future version adds such services (for example, advertising or in-app purchases), we will update this Privacy Policy and the App Store / Google Play privacy information before that version is released.

5. Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

6. Your rights

Since we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by uninstalling the Game.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
